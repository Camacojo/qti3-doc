This page is for product managers and decision-makers weighing whether to adopt
QTI. The short version: **QTI protects your investment in assessment content by
making it portable.**

## The problem it solves

Assessment content is expensive to author and quality-assure. Without a standard,
each item is locked into the tool that created it. Switching vendors, combining
item banks, or delivering the same content in a new player means re-authoring
everything — and re-validating it.

## What you gain

- **Vendor independence.** Content authored to QTI can be delivered by any
  QTI-capable player. You are not locked to one supplier.
- **Reuse across contexts.** The same item bank can feed formative practice,
  summative exams, and third-party platforms.
- **Longevity.** An open, versioned standard outlives individual products, so
  content stays usable for years.
- **An ecosystem of tools.** Authoring tools, delivery engines, and open-source
  libraries already speak QTI — you can assemble a stack rather than build it.
- **Accessibility built in.** QTI has first-class support for describing
  alternative content and candidate needs, helping meet accessibility
  obligations. See [Accessibility](/concepts/accessibility/).

## What to be realistic about

- **"QTI-compliant" is a spectrum.** Tools support different subsets of
  interactions and features. Interoperability in practice depends on the overlap
  between the tools you use — always test round-trips with real content.
- **Delivery is still yours to build.** QTI describes content and scoring, not
  the test-taking experience, session handling, or reporting.
- **Migration has a cost.** Moving legacy content into QTI is a project, though a
  one-time one that pays back through portability.

## Where Kennisnet fits

Kennisnet maintains the open-source [`kennisnet/php-qti3`](/onboarding/open-source-packages/)
library for working with QTI 3.0 on the server, complementing browser rendering
via [`@citolab/qti-components`](/onboarding/open-source-packages/). Together they
let teams adopt QTI without starting from zero.
