The `qti-gap-match-interaction` presents a passage of content containing one or more gaps, together with a set of draggable text choices. The candidate answers by dragging each choice into the gap where it belongs.

## Purpose

Use this interaction when you want the candidate to complete a passage by filling inline gaps with the correct words or phrases.

- Fill-in-the-blank exercises where the answers come from a fixed, shared pool of choices.
- Cloze-style reading comprehension tasks.
- Vocabulary or grammar drills where distractor choices are provided alongside the correct ones.

Each gap is a drop target embedded directly in the flow of the content, so the surrounding text gives context for what belongs in each position. A single choice can be reused across multiple gaps by setting its `match-max` appropriately.

## Place in the hierarchy

- **Parent:** the interaction lives in the item body (`qti-item-body`), typically within block-level content such as a paragraph or blockquote.
- **Children:**
  - `qti-gap-text` (text) or `qti-gap-img` (image) elements are the draggable choices. Each has an `identifier` and a `match-max` controlling how many gaps it may be dropped into. These are usually declared before the passage content.
  - `qti-gap` elements are the inline drop targets, placed within the content of the passage. Each has an `identifier`.
- **Variable binding:** it binds to a response variable (named by `response-identifier`) declared with a `qti-response-declaration`. The response has cardinality `multiple` and base-type `directedPair`, where each pair associates a `qti-gap-text` identifier with a `qti-gap` identifier (in that order: gapText then gap).

## Example

<Example title="Complete the sentence by dragging words into the gaps">

```xml
<qti-response-declaration identifier="RESPONSE" cardinality="multiple" base-type="directedPair">
  <qti-correct-response>
    <qti-value>W1 G1</qti-value>
    <qti-value>W2 G2</qti-value>
  </qti-correct-response>
</qti-response-declaration>

<qti-item-body>
  <qti-gap-match-interaction response-identifier="RESPONSE" shuffle="true" max-associations="0">
    <qti-prompt>Drag the correct word into each gap.</qti-prompt>
    <qti-gap-text identifier="W1" match-max="1">photosynthesis</qti-gap-text>
    <qti-gap-text identifier="W2" match-max="1">respiration</qti-gap-text>
    <qti-gap-text identifier="W3" match-max="1">evaporation</qti-gap-text>
    <blockquote>
      <p>
        Plants convert light energy into chemical energy through
        <qti-gap identifier="G1"/>, while cells release energy from
        glucose through <qti-gap identifier="G2"/>.
      </p>
    </blockquote>
  </qti-gap-match-interaction>
</qti-item-body>
```

</Example>
