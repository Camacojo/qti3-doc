The `qti-graphic-order-interaction` presents an image with a set of predefined hotspots that the candidate must place into a specific order. It is the graphic counterpart of the `qti-order-interaction`: instead of ordering text choices, the candidate sequences regions of an image.

## Purpose

Use this interaction when the ordering task is inherently visual and the things being ordered are areas of a picture rather than words or phrases. Typical uses include:

- Sequencing stages of a process shown in a diagram (for example, ordering the steps of a life cycle).
- Ranking labelled regions of a map, chart, or illustration.
- Arranging parts of an image chronologically or by size.

The candidate assigns a rank to each hotspot, and the resulting ordered list of hotspot identifiers is captured in the bound response variable.

## Place in the hierarchy

- **Parent:** appears within the `qti-item-body` (directly or nested inside block-level content).
- **Children:**
  - Exactly one HTML `object`, `img`, or `picture` element that references the background image and defines its dimensions.
  - One `qti-hotspot-choice` element for each selectable region. Each hotspot declares an `identifier`, a `shape`, and the matching `coords`.
- **Binds to:** a `qti-response-declaration` with cardinality `ordered` and base-type `identifier`. The declared correct response is the ordered sequence of hotspot identifiers.

The child `qti-hotspot-choice` elements each carry their own attributes, most importantly `identifier`, `shape` (for example `rect`, `circle`, `poly`, or `ellipse`), and `coords` (the coordinate values matching the chosen shape).

## Example

<Example title="Order the stages of the water cycle shown on the diagram">

```xml
<qti-response-declaration identifier="RESPONSE" cardinality="ordered" base-type="identifier">
  <qti-correct-response>
    <qti-value>evaporation</qti-value>
    <qti-value>condensation</qti-value>
    <qti-value>precipitation</qti-value>
    <qti-value>collection</qti-value>
  </qti-correct-response>
</qti-response-declaration>

<qti-item-body>
  <qti-graphic-order-interaction response-identifier="RESPONSE">
    <qti-prompt>Place the stages of the water cycle in the correct order.</qti-prompt>
    <img src="images/water-cycle.png" alt="Diagram of the water cycle" width="600" height="400"/>
    <qti-hotspot-choice identifier="evaporation" shape="circle" coords="120,300,40"/>
    <qti-hotspot-choice identifier="condensation" shape="circle" coords="300,80,40"/>
    <qti-hotspot-choice identifier="precipitation" shape="circle" coords="470,180,40"/>
    <qti-hotspot-choice identifier="collection" shape="rect" coords="380,330,520,390"/>
  </qti-graphic-order-interaction>
</qti-item-body>
```

</Example>

:::note
A live demo is omitted here: outside a full item/player context the

against a collapsed host, so ordering does not display correctly. See a working
