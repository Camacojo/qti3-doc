`qti-selection` and `qti-ordering` sit inside a
[section](/reference/test/assessment-section/) and shape what the candidate
actually sees: which children are chosen, and in what order. Together they turn a
fixed item bank into randomised forms.

## qti-selection — pick a subset

Chooses a number of the section's children at delivery.

<AttributesTable attributes={[
  { name: 'select', type: 'integer', required: true, description: 'How many child items/sections to select.' },
  { name: 'with-replacement', type: 'boolean', default: 'false', description: 'Whether the same child may be selected more than once.' },
]} caption="qti-selection attributes" />

Mark a child `required="true"` (on the [item ref](/reference/test/assessment-item-ref/))
to force its inclusion in every selection.

## qti-ordering — shuffle

Randomises the order of the (selected) children.

<AttributesTable attributes={[
  { name: 'shuffle', type: 'boolean', default: 'false', description: 'When true, the order of children is randomised for each candidate. Children marked fixed keep their position.' },
]} caption="qti-ordering attributes" />

## Example

<Example title="Draw 5 of 20 items, shuffled">
```xml
<qti-assessment-section identifier="pool" title="Question pool" visible="false">
  <qti-selection select="5"/>
  <qti-ordering shuffle="true"/>
  <!-- 20 qti-assessment-item-ref children -->
</qti-assessment-section>
```
</Example>
