Accessibility is built into QTI 3.0 rather than bolted on. The standard lets an
item carry **alternative content**, and lets a candidate's **needs and
preferences** decide which content is delivered — so one item can serve many
learners.

## Two halves that work together

- **PNP — Personal Needs & Preferences.** A description of what a candidate
  needs: larger text, spoken audio, a glossary, extra time, simplified language.
  It travels with the session, not the item.
- **Catalog.** A block inside an item that provides *alternatives* for its
  content — a spoken version, a glossary definition, sign-language video,
  keyword translations. Each alternative is tagged so a player can pick the right
  one for the candidate's PNP.

At delivery, the player matches the PNP against the catalog and substitutes or
adds content accordingly.

## How a catalog is wired up

Content that has alternatives references a catalog by `data-catalog-idref`; the
`qti-catalog-info` block holds the alternatives, each in a `qti-card` for a
`support` type such as `glossary-on-screen` or `spoken`.

<Example title="A glossary alternative for a term">
```xml
<p>The <span data-catalog-idref="cat-photosynthesis">photosynthesis</span> process…</p>

<qti-catalog-info>
  <qti-catalog id="cat-photosynthesis">
    <qti-card support="glossary-on-screen">
      <qti-html-content>
        <div>The process by which plants convert light into chemical energy.</div>
      </qti-html-content>
    </qti-card>
  </qti-catalog>
</qti-catalog-info>
```
</Example>

## Relationship to APIP

Earlier work on accessible assessment was standardised as **APIP** (Accessible
Portable Item Protocol). QTI 3.0 absorbs those ideas into the core catalog and
PNP model, so most accessibility needs are met with standard QTI rather than a
separate profile.

## Why product teams care

Supporting PNP and catalogs is often the difference between meeting accessibility
obligations with a single item bank versus maintaining parallel accessible copies.
See [Why QTI 3.0?](/onboarding/why-qti/).
