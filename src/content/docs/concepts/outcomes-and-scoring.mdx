**Outcomes** are the results an item or test produces. The most familiar is
`SCORE`, but outcomes can also drive [feedback](/concepts/feedback/), record a
maximum score, or flag whether an item was answered.

## Outcome variables

Outcomes are declared with [`qti-outcome-declaration`](/reference/processing/outcome-declaration/)
and written by [response processing](/concepts/response-processing/). Conventions
you will see everywhere:

- `SCORE` — the marks awarded (`float`).
- `MAXSCORE` — the maximum possible (`float`), useful for normalisation.
- `FEEDBACK` — an identifier selecting which feedback to show.

Give outcomes a sensible `qti-default-value` (usually `0` for `SCORE`) — numeric
outcomes implicitly initialize to `0` even without one, but declaring the
default makes the intent explicit; non-numeric outcomes otherwise start as
NULL.

## Scoring models

- **Right/wrong (dichotomous).** Full marks or none — the
  [`match_correct`](/reference/processing/built-in-templates/) template.
- **Partial credit.** A [mapping](/reference/processing/response-declaration/)
  awards marks per correct element — the `map_response` template. Good for
  multiple-response and match items.
- **Custom.** Any logic you can express with
  [rules and operators](/reference/processing/rules/).

<Example title="Declaring SCORE and MAXSCORE">
```xml
<qti-outcome-declaration identifier="SCORE" cardinality="single" base-type="float">
  <qti-default-value><qti-value>0</qti-value></qti-default-value>
</qti-outcome-declaration>
<qti-outcome-declaration identifier="MAXSCORE" cardinality="single" base-type="float">
  <qti-default-value><qti-value>1</qti-value></qti-default-value>
</qti-outcome-declaration>
```
</Example>

## From item to test

Item outcomes do not automatically become a test score. At the test level,
**outcome processing** (`qti-outcome-processing`) aggregates them — most commonly
summing each item's `SCORE` into a test total using the
[`qti-sum`](/reference/processing/operators/) operator over all items.

This separation means an item's scoring is self-contained and reusable, while the
test decides how to combine results. See
[Assessment test & sections](/concepts/assessment-test-and-sections/).
