An **assessment test** (`qti-assessment-test`) is an ordered collection of
[items](/concepts/assessment-item/) delivered as one sitting. Its job is
structure and sequencing: which items, in what order, grouped how, and how their
scores combine.

## The test hierarchy

```
qti-assessment-test
└── qti-test-part           (one or more; sets navigation & submission mode)
    └── qti-assessment-section   (groups items; can nest)
        └── qti-assessment-item-ref   (points to an item by href)
```

- **Test part** — a phase of the test. It sets the **navigation mode**
  (`linear` = forced order, or `nonlinear` = free navigation) and the
  **submission mode** (`individual` per item, or `simultaneous` at the end).
- **Section** — groups items for organisation, and controls **selection** (pick
  N of M) and **ordering** (shuffle or fixed). Sections can nest.
- **Item reference** — items are not inlined; a section references them by
  `href`, so the same item can be reused across tests.

## Selection and ordering

Two child elements on a section shape what the candidate sees:

- `qti-selection` — e.g. select 5 items at random from a pool of 20.
- `qti-ordering` — e.g. shuffle the selected items.

This is how you build randomised forms from an item bank.

<Example title="A test with one part and one shuffled section">
```xml
<qti-assessment-test identifier="quiz-geo" title="Geography quiz">
  <qti-test-part identifier="part1" navigation-mode="linear" submission-mode="individual">
    <qti-assessment-section identifier="sec1" title="Capitals" visible="true">
      <qti-ordering shuffle="true"/>
      <qti-assessment-item-ref identifier="I1" href="items/capital-nl.xml"/>
      <qti-assessment-item-ref identifier="I2" href="items/capital-be.xml"/>
      <qti-assessment-item-ref identifier="I3" href="items/capital-de.xml"/>
    </qti-assessment-section>
  </qti-test-part>
</qti-assessment-test>
```
</Example>

## Aggregating scores

Each item produces its own [outcomes](/concepts/outcomes-and-scoring/). At the
test level, **outcome processing** (`qti-outcome-processing`) aggregates them —
for example summing every item `SCORE` into a test-level total. This is the
test-level counterpart of item [response processing](/concepts/response-processing/).
